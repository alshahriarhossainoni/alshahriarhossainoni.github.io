* Liquidity Analysis
* Bookmap Heatmap
* Footprint Charts
* Institutional Trading Concepts
* Risk Management

---

## Trading Philosophy

Markets operate as auctions.

Price moves in search of liquidity and value. Instead of predicting the market, I focus on understanding:

* Where liquidity is resting
* Where value is accepted or rejected
* Buyer and seller aggression
* Market imbalances
* Auction failures
* Institutional participation
